Privacy Risk Register — How to Use

Self-directed project: Cyber Risk Advisory & Privacy Tech — case study on a healthcare/fitness mobile app

  1. Go to the Risk Register tab. Six example risks are already filled in — these are samples, not real findings.
  2. To add your own risk: fill in a new row — Risk ID, Data Category, Risk Description, Likelihood, Impact, Recommended Control, Residual Likelihood, Residual Impact.
  3. Likelihood, Impact, Residual Likelihood and Residual Impact are dropdowns — click the cell and choose Low, Medium, or High.
  4. Don't type into Inherent Risk or Residual Risk — they calculate automatically from the Risk Matrix tab and are color-coded green/amber/red.
  5. The Summary tab totals everything automatically — no manual updates needed.
  6. The Risk Matrix tab shows the scoring logic this register is built on — reference only.
