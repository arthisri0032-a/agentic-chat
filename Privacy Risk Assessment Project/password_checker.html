Why it matters

  • Short, common, or pattern-based passwords (like "password1" or "qwerty123") can be cracked in seconds with freely available tools.
  • Length matters more than complexity — a long passphrase is often stronger and easier to remember than a short, symbol-heavy password.
  • Reusing the same password across sites means one breach can compromise every account that shares it.
