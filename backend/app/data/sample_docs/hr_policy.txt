Aurora Robotics — Employee Handbook (HR Policy Excerpt)

Remote Work
Aurora Robotics operates a hybrid work policy. Engineering and design staff are expected in the
Bengaluru office on Tuesdays and Thursdays; all other days may be worked remotely unless a manager
requests otherwise for a specific project. Fully remote arrangements require VP approval and are
reviewed every six months.

Paid Time Off
Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of paid time off per month, totalling 21 days per year, in
addition to 10 public holidays. PTO requests should be submitted through the HR portal at least five
business days in advance, except for sick leave, which may be taken with same-day notice. Unused PTO
of up to 5 days may be carried over into the next calendar year; the remainder is paid out at the
December payroll cycle.

Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 26 weeks of paid parental leave; secondary caregivers receive 4
weeks. Leave can begin up to 4 weeks before the expected due date and must be requested through HR at
least 30 days in advance where possible.

Expense Reimbursement
Business expenses under ₹5,000 can be self-approved by the employee's manager through the expense
portal. Expenses above that threshold require director-level approval. Reimbursements are processed
within 10 business days of submission, provided a valid receipt is attached.

Performance Reviews
Performance reviews are conducted twice a year, in April and October. Ratings feed into the annual
compensation review in November. Employees can request an off-cycle review after 6 months in a new
role by speaking with their manager and HR business partner.

Code of Conduct
Employees are expected to disclose any conflicts of interest, including outside employment or equity
stakes in competing companies, to HR within 30 days of the conflict arising. Violations are handled
according to the disciplinary process described in Section 9 of the full handbook.
