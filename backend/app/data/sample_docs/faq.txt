Aurora Robotics — Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What products does Aurora Robotics make?
A: Aurora Robotics makes the RoboArm X1 desktop robotic arm, the AMC-2 motion controller, and the
Aurora SDK for controlling Aurora hardware from Python and C++.

Q: Where is Aurora Robotics based?
A: Aurora Robotics is headquartered in Bengaluru, India, with a smaller hardware lab in Austin, Texas
focused on motor and gearbox testing.

Q: How do I get support for a defective unit?
A: Open a support ticket at support.aurora-robotics.example with your order number and a description
of the issue. Hardware covered under the 24-month warranty is repaired or replaced at no cost; see the
product manual for what the warranty covers.

Q: Does Aurora offer an educational discount?
A: Yes. Accredited schools and universities receive a 30% discount on the RoboArm X1 when purchasing
through the education portal. A minimum order of 3 units is required for the discount to apply.

Q: Can the RoboArm X1 be controlled from ROS?
A: Yes, a community-maintained ROS2 driver wraps the Aurora SDK. It is not officially supported by
Aurora Robotics, but the SDK's documented serial protocol is stable across firmware versions, so the
driver has historically required little maintenance.

Q: What is Aurora Robotics' return policy?
A: Unused hardware in original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund
minus a 5% restocking fee. Custom or bulk orders over 10 units are non-returnable.

Q: How do I contact sales for a bulk order?
A: Email sales@aurora-robotics.example or use the "Request a Quote" form on the pricing page. Bulk
orders of 10 or more units typically receive a response within 2 business days.
